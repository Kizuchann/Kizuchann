## About

I'm **Akihiko K.**, usually known online as **Kizuchann** or
**Kizuki**.

I'm a developer who enjoys building things from scratch and
figuring out how they work along the way.

Most of my work revolves around **Python, Linux and automation**,
but I don't like limiting myself to a single stack. I build
tools, applications, bots and other IT projects depending on
the problem I'm trying to solve.

I usually care more about making something work well than
following a particular technology or trend.

**Languages:** Russian · English
